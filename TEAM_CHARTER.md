2. App Summary
Mental Health is a web platform built for University students seeking mental health support and resources. Users can create an account, browse and save upcoming wellness events, and access a directory of mental health professionals (doctors) with their specializations and contact details. Admins manage events and moderate user accounts, including promoting or demoting other users to admin status.
